Delete your Savorly account

Last updated 16 October 2026

You can delete your account for Savorly (the website savorly.cooking and the Savorly apps for iOS and Android) at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app or on the website

  1. Open Savorly and sign in.
  2. Go to My kitchen.
  3. Choose Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password and choose Permanently delete my account. If you sign in only with Apple or Google, confirm by signing in with it once more.

Your account is deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

By email

If you can't use the app, email privacy@savorly.cooking from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account", and include your username if you know it. We may ask you to confirm it's you. We delete the account within 30 days and tell you by email when it's done.

What is deleted

Immediately and permanently, from our database:

  • your account: email address, name, username, password hash, profile photo, bio, food preferences and plan details;
  • your connections to Apple and Google sign-in (we also revoke Savorly's access with Apple) and every signed-in session, on all your devices;
  • your recipes, published and drafts, with their read-aloud audio and the reviews, saves and meal-plan entries other cooks made for them;
  • your reviews and ratings, reports, saved recipes and cookbooks, follows, meal plans, grocery list and pantry;
  • your place in a household, with the items you added to its shared grocery list (if you owned it, the member who has been in it longest takes it over);
  • your devices' notification tokens, your notification settings, and the record of how you used the app;
  • your sous-chef conversations and transcripts, your AI requests and their results, and the photos you showed the sous-chef (also removed from storage).

If you have a paid plan, we cancel the subscription first.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups. Our database provider keeps a point-in-time history for up to 30 days so we can recover from failures. Deleted information leaves it after that.
  • Uploaded photos and videos. They are stored as separate files at addresses that are hard to guess. Deleting your account removes them from Savorly at once, and the files themselves are deleted from storage about a day later. A photo that another cook's adaptation of one of your recipes still shows stays until it no longer does.
  • Spending records. The cost and date of read-aloud audio you generated stay in our records of AI spending, no longer linked to you.
  • Moderation records. If our administrators acted on your account or content (for example a suspension or a removed recipe), the record of that action, which can include your name and email address, is kept to protect the community.
  • Payments. If you had a paid plan, Stripe keeps payment and invoice records for as long as financial and tax law requires.
  • Logs. Our hosting provider's request logs expire within a few days.
  • Copies other cooks made. A recipe another cook adapted into their own draft is theirs and stays with them.

More about what we collect and why is in our privacy policy. Questions: privacy@savorly.cooking.
